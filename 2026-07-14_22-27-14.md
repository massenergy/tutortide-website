## 2. Content architecture — how each page answers the funders

### 2.1 Nyokavi — unified narrative (Netherlands + DIV Fund)

One scroll that hits every scoring lever of both funders. Positioning line (from collateral):
**"Bionic M-Pesa Data Refinery — mapping the Invisible GDP."** Section arc:

1. **Hero** — "Mapping the Invisible GDP." Nyokavi's deterministic engine de-couples personal draws
   from true operating margins and generates a tamper-proof Bankability Scorecard. Headline stats:
   **33-min** edge-to-insight window · **<$850** per 200K daily txns/month · **200,000+** merchants
   targeted in Phase One.
2. **The problem** (NL Relevance / market failure) — billions in daily M-Pesa commerce co-mingled in
   personal accounts, invisible to banks, insurers, fintechs and the state; the "Invisible Tax"; a
   business-climate + market-entry constraint (NL Focus B).
3. **How the engine works** (DIV rigor) — the edge→cloud pipeline: SMS/WhatsApp edge event →
   WorkManager cache + crypto signing → Pub/Sub → GKE/Dataflow regex refinery → Memorystore dedup →
   structured JSON (Bankability Score + Invisible Tax). Strict 3-layer schema (Observed facts /
   Derived metrics / Predictions), zero-behaviour-change, on-device.
4. **The three products** (evidence of rigor) — **Invisible Tax Index™** (KES/mo lost to owner draws,
   fees, Fuliza dependency); **Bankability Scorecard** (5 pillars: Revenue Stability, Profitability,
   Cash Discipline, Debt Exposure, Record Quality — no paper trail); **Sector Intelligence Layer**
   (classifies micro-food retail, grocery, industrial extraction, personal accounts).
5. **Verified pilot case studies** (DIV evidence + NL relevance) — the four company-verified MSME
   studies, attributed as its own verified M-Pesa data: BIZ001 Micro-Food Kibanda (KES 123,825 rev,
   71.4% GP, 27.5% Fuliza); BIZ002 Kiosk Green Grocer (KES 495,180 rev, 73.8% GP, 475% growth);
   BIZ003 Industrial Quarry (KES 28.8M rev, +13.8% true GP after reclass, KES 4.07M owner income
   isolated); BIZ004 Food+Grocery Till (KES 476,874 rev, 27% net margin, 4,999 txns).
6. **Cost-effectiveness & durable scale** (DIV thesis) — marginal cost per merchant → ~0 (zero-CAC,
   zero infra); scale on mobile-money ubiquity (38M Kenyan users, cited) across sub-Saharan Africa.
7. **Systemic path / business climate** (NL Focus A+B, Systemic) — **proposed 3-tier roadmap**:
   Tier 1 KNJF sector-aggregator pilot (intermediation aggregating informal SMEs, Focus B); Tier 2
   UNIDO standards validation; Tier 3 **State Bridge** to MSEA / State Dept for MSMEs via API
   (administrative-compliance optimization, Focus A). All labeled *proposed*.
8. **Who it serves** — enterprise verticals from the collateral: Banking & MFIs, Digital Lending &
   FinTech, Insurance & Micro-InsureTech, Wealth Management. (Targets: Impact Networks, FSD Kenya,
   institutional banks, MFIs.)
9. **Inclusion** (NL requirement) — benefits to women, youth, and marginalized informal traders.
10. **Additionality & sustainability** (NL) — the market failure it addresses; why impact continues
    past a grant. **Research-mode model, stated lightly:** the borrower's initial report is **free**,
    with a **small KES-200 monthly token** to keep benefits — kept subtle (no pricing tables/checkout;
    this is a research-stage funder page, not a sales page).
11. **Evidence & rigor** — the data-schema "moat"; **OGMA Risk & Analytics (USA)** shown as a
    *proposed* statistical-validation partner (SR 11-7 alignment) — NOT as a signed framework.
12. **CTA** — "Partner with us / contact" (positioning; no launch — R&D).

### 2.2 Tundamoto — Netherlands call (agri-export traceability)

1. **Hero** — farm-to-Europe digital traceability that cuts export-clearance friction for smallholders.
2. **The problem** (NL Relevance) — fragmented horticultural value chains; phytosanitary/export
   compliance friction; weak SME→buyer linkages (Focus B); regulatory/admin cost (Focus A).
3. **The platform** — the five-sided model (land owners, financiers, off-takers, EPC, operators),
   reframed as an export-enabling traceability + market-linkage system.
4. **Government integration roadmap** (NL Focus A — *proposed* partners) — KEPHIS (phytosanitary/export
   cert API), KenInvest/KenTrade (single-window expansion, trade-doc automation), HCD (export-license
   compliance workflow).
5. **Market entry & expansion** (NL Focus B) — aggregating smallholders, linking to off-takers/export
   buyers, lowering entry barriers for agri-export SMEs.
6. **Systemic change, sustainability, inclusion** — smallholders, women, youth.
7. **Cost-effectiveness** (NL Budget) — how it delivers impact per shilling.
8. **CTA** — partner/contact (positioning; no launch).
