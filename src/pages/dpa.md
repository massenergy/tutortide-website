# Data Processing Agreement (DPA)

**Effective Date:** October 18, 2025

This Data Processing Agreement ("DPA") is entered into between **Tutortide Limited** ("Processor"), a company registered in Kenya, and the educational institution ("Controller") that has subscribed to the AI Student Companion services ("Services"). This DPA is incorporated into the main Terms and Conditions agreed upon by the Controller.

## 1. Definitions

- **"Personal Data"**: Any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person, specifically the data of Students, Parents, and Teachers as processed by the Services.
- **"Data Protection Laws"**: All applicable data protection and privacy laws, including but not limited to GDPR, FERPA, COPPA, CCPA, and the Kenya Data Protection Act.
- **"Controller"**: The educational institution that determines the purposes and means of processing Personal Data.
- **"Processor"**: Tutortide Limited, which processes Personal Data on behalf of the Controller.
- **"Student"**: Any individual under the age of 18 whose data is processed by the Services.

## 2. Roles and Responsibilities

2.1. **Processor's Obligations:**

- Tutortide Limited agrees to process Personal Data only on the documented instructions of the Controller.
- We will ensure that all personnel authorized to process Personal Data are bound by a duty of confidentiality.
- We will implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to ensure a level of security appropriate to the risk, including data encryption, access controls, and regular security assessments.

2.2. **Controller's Obligations:**

- The Controller warrants that it has a lawful basis for processing all Personal Data submitted to the Services.
- **Crucially, the Controller assumes the full responsibility for obtaining and documenting verifiable parental or guardian consent for any Student under the age of 18 before creating an account or submitting their Personal Data to the Services.** This consent must be obtained in a manner that complies with all applicable Data Protection Laws (including COPPA, GDPR, and the Kenya DPA).
- The Controller is responsible for managing and verifying the accuracy of the consent records.

## 3. Data Subject Rights

The Processor will assist the Controller in fulfilling its obligation to respond to requests from data subjects to exercise their rights (e.g., access, rectification, erasure) by providing the necessary tools and data access functionalities within the Services.

## 4. Data Breach Notification

The Processor will notify the Controller without undue delay after becoming aware of a Personal Data breach. We will provide the Controller with sufficient information to meet any legal reporting obligations.

## 5. Data Transfers

Personal Data will be stored and processed within secure cloud infrastructure. Any international transfers of data will be conducted in accordance with Data Protection Laws.

## 6. Term and Termination

This DPA will remain in effect as long as the Processor processes Personal Data on behalf of the Controller. Upon termination of the Services, the Processor will delete or return all Personal Data at the Controller's choice, unless required by law to retain it.
